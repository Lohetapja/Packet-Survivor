How to Play

You are a Defender Packet inside a compromised company network. Move around, avoid threats, collect telemetry, and upgrade your defensive tools. Survive as many waves as possible.

⌨️

Move

Use WASD or the arrow keys.

🛡️

Defend

Your tools auto-activate. No aiming needed — keep moving and let them work.

🟢

Collect

Defeated threats drop telemetry. Gather it to fill your level bar.

⬆️

Upgrade

Each level-up, choose 1 of 3 defensive upgrades or unlock a new tool.

⏸️

Pause

Press Space or the pause button any time.

⚠️

Survive

Threats damage you on contact. Reach 0 health and the network is breached.
